# Widget design

Use a system font stack, a 4 px rhythm, gaps of 8, 12, 16 and 24, radii of 8 to 12, 1 px borders and flat surfaces. Controls are 32 to 40 px high. Support right-to-left when the text is Arabic, and narrow widths down to 360 px. Respect prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme.
